- **Form contract**: Both modes use the same inquiry endpoint; name, normalized phone, message, type and privacy acknowledgement are required. - **Durable lead identity**: D1 acceptance has a UUID reference, creation time, locale, notice version/acknowledgement time, unique idempotency-key hash and payload hash. Provider delivery is a separate recoverable status, never the basis for visitor success. (PRD §§9/11.4; constitution II/VII.)
- **Security/retry contract**: Server-side Turnstile, allowlisted validation, rate limits and parameterized storage remain mandatory. A refreshed verification token alone does not change lead content or the retry identity; identical replay returns the original reference, while conflicting lead content returns 409. Numeric limits and payload comparison rules are planning outputs, with boundary/concurrency tests required. (PRD P0-F005, §§9/11.2–11.4; constitution VI/VII.)
- **Notification recovery gate**: A hosted Phase 1 demonstration may use controlled, labeled notification-provider simulation to prove persisted failure status and authorized recovery. Actual hosted D1/Turnstile evidence cannot be replaced by simulation. Live provider/recipient delivery is finalized in Phase 2 and verified before launch; it is not an added Phase 1 blocker. (PRD §10 Phases 1/2, P0-F005; constitution VII/VIII.)
- **Quality/preview evidence**: Both locales require the five viewport widths, manual accessibility review and measured performance baselines already specified. Lab results are not field percentiles; preview uses synthetic leads, isolated bindings and no indexing of draft content. Profiles/budgets and the sustained-notification-failure trigger are defined in planning; fixed PRD thresholds remain unchanged. (PRD §§8–10/12; constitution IV–VIII.)
- **Approval dependencies**: Business facts, contacts, asset rights, bilingual editorial/legal text, vendor/data-location clearance, retention/backups and named operational owners remain external gates. Planning can proceed with recorded dependencies; Phase 1 completion and production authorization cannot be inferred from this specification. (PRD §§4/13–15; constitution I/VI/VIII.)

## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

### User Story 1 - Send a traceable inquiry or quotation (Priority: P1)

As a residential or commercial buyer, I can reach the contact prototype, send a general inquiry or quotation request in Arabic or English, and receive a reference only when my request is safely stored.

**Why this priority**: A credible website must prove that customers can reach KEN without silently losing their requests. Persistence precedes decorative enhancement.

**Independent Test**: With staging dependencies configured and synthetic data, directly open each localized Contact route and exercise both modes without requiring finished marketing pages or a working email provider.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** `/ar/contact` or `/en/contact`, **When** a visitor selects either mode, supplies valid required fields, completes human verification and explicitly acknowledges the notice, **Then** the request is durably stored once with locale, reference and timestamp, and an accessible localized confirmation receives focus.
2. **Given** a quotation link carrying a valid service identifier, **When** Contact opens, **Then** quotation and the service are preselected but editable; optional building details are available and no price is promised.
3. **Given** missing, malformed or out-of-range input, **When** submission is attempted, **Then** localized inline corrections identify fields, focus goes to the first invalid field, the draft remains, and no lead is inserted; bypassing browser validation gives the same rejection.
4. **Given** a network interruption or storage outage, **When** submission fails, **Then** the visitor sees a localized unsuccessful/pending-unknown outcome and retry guidance, retains all entered fields, and never receives a false stored confirmation.
5. **Given** an accepted request whose response was lost, **When** the unchanged request is retried with the same submission identity, **Then** the original reference is returned without another lead; different lead content with the same identity is rejected as a conflict.
6. **Given** either locale, **When** a token expires or the visitor exceeds the configured request limit, **Then** human-verification refresh or cooldown guidance is localized, input is preserved, and no rejected request is stored.

### User Story 2 - Recover a lead when notification fails (Priority: P1)

As an authorized KEN lead operator, I can retrieve accepted requests and recover failed notifications even when the notification service is unavailable.

**Why this priority**: Visitor confirmation is useful only if operations can act on the durable request.

**Independent Test**: Force notification failure for synthetic Arabic and English leads, then retrieve and replay them through restricted operational access, without a dashboard or completed site.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a stored lead, **When** notification fails or times out, **Then** the visitor retains successful confirmation, the lead remains retrievable, and a linked pending/failed notification status and safe failure evidence exist.
2. **Given** a stored lead and configured notification test path, **When** a Phase 1 simulated outcome is exercised, **Then** the outcome is explicitly labeled and linked to the lead; when live delivery is enabled, the approved test recipient can match the notification to that lead and provider acceptance status is recorded. Live delivery remains required before launch.
3. **Given** pending/failed notifications, **When** the authorized retry or documented replay runs after service restoration, **Then** the existing lead is delivered or explicitly escalated, attempt evidence is updated, and no additional inquiry is created.
4. **Given** an interrupted process after storage, including before a delivery attempt is recorded, **When** daily reconciliation runs, **Then** the accepted lead is found as pending/unattempted and recoverable; every failed-notification inquiry is visible to the designated operator within one business day.
5. **Given** an unauthorized visitor, **When** they attempt to list, read or update inquiries, **Then** no personal data or lead record is disclosed.

### User Story 3 - Browse a premium bilingual foundation (Priority: P2)

As an Arabic-speaking or English-speaking buyer, I can browse a readable initial homepage and shared navigation on my device, choose my language, and find the on-site quotation/contact journey.

**Why this priority**: Directional correctness, accessibility and a clear next step establish trust and provide a reusable foundation for later content.

**Independent Test**: Inspect Home, Contact, privacy and preview preparation states in both locales using fixture content, at each required width, with keyboard, screen reader and reduced motion.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a first visit to `/`, **When** it resolves, **Then** `/ar/` is shown with Arabic language/RTL; a deliberate `/en/` visit remains English/LTR after refresh.
2. **Given** any paired Phase 1 route, **When** the language switcher is used, **Then** the same page and safe quotation context remain available in the other locale, with translated navigation, metadata and interaction states.
3. **Given** mobile navigation, **When** it is opened by touch or keyboard and closed with Escape, **Then** links are reachable, expanded state is announced, and focus returns to the trigger.
4. **Given** the initial homepage at 360, 390, 768, 1024 or 1440 CSS pixels, **When** either locale is inspected, **Then** the heading, hero and quotation/contact CTA remain readable, with no horizontal overflow or clipped Arabic text.
5. **Given** reduced motion, animation failure or failed imagery, **When** the homepage is viewed, **Then** readable content, layout space, text alternatives and primary CTAs remain available without hover or animation dependence.
6. **Given** unavailable project evidence or unverified contact details, **When** the preview is viewed, **Then** an explicit preparation/empty state appears or the unavailable action is suppressed; invented numbers, claims and projects never appear.

### User Story 4 - Review a verifiable preview (Priority: P2)

As QA or a stakeholder, I can open a traceable preview and inspect critical journey evidence and outstanding dependencies before approving later work.

**Why this priority**: A repeatable preview distinguishes demonstrated behavior from pending access, content and production approvals.

**Independent Test**: Review the preview URL/build identity, check report, environment-separation evidence, dependency register and a repeatable end-to-end synthetic lead demonstration.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a candidate change, **When** any mandatory quality check fails, **Then** it cannot be marked preview-ready; a passing build is traceable to a source revision and preview URL.
2. **Given** the deployed preview, **When** Home, Contact and privacy routes are opened directly and refreshed in either locale, **Then** meaningful localized initial HTML is present; unknown routes return a localized 404 with safe navigation.
3. **Given** preview credentials and notification configuration, **When** a test inquiry is submitted, **Then** it reaches only staging storage and, if live delivery is enabled, approved test recipients, never production data or an unapproved business inbox.
4. **Given** missing external access or approvals, **When** Phase 1 readiness is reported, **Then** blocked checks identify the missing dependency, owner and gate; notification-provider simulation may prove the Phase 1 recovery behavior, but never counts as live provider delivery or replaces hosted D1/Turnstile evidence.

### Edge Cases

- Required name lengths 1/2/100/101 and message lengths 9/10/2,000/2,001; invalid phone/email; optional fields omitted; privacy acknowledgement false/missing; invalid enum/type or notice version: reject invalid requests without storage and preserve correct input.
- Malformed service/type query context: ignore unsafe preselection and keep the form usable; submitted invalid values are rejected independently. A valid service selection can be changed or cleared.
- Rapid double submission, concurrent identical retries and lost acceptance response: one stored lead and original reference; materially changed content with the same identity: conflict with safe retry guidance.
- Missing/expired/invalid bot token or failed verification service: fail closed, retain draft and offer a refreshed verification/retry path.
- Oversized payload, unsupported fields, injection-like text and sustained request burst: enforce configured bounds and safe handling; no executable content, leaked internals or unauthorized record access.
- Storage failure and a process interruption after persistence: failure never fabricates acceptance; any genuinely accepted lead remains discoverable for recovery.
- No live email provider: a labeled notification simulation may prove recovery on the hosted Phase 1 preview; live delivery remains a later gate. Missing hosted D1/Turnstile or authorized operator access blocks Phase 1 integration/recovery acceptance.
- Storage commits but the process stops before notification status/attempt update: reconciliation still discovers the inquiry as pending/unattempted; no accepted lead can fall outside the recovery inventory.
- Missing translation, unsupported locale, unknown route or missing image: fail invalid builds; runtime fallback uses a localized explanation/safe home or correct 404, never raw keys or fabricated evidence.
- Unverified phone/WhatsApp or unapproved privacy text: suppress direct actions; preview uses explicitly marked synthetic testing only until real-data approvals exist.
- Blocked enhancements or unavailable JavaScript: initial content and ordinary navigation remain readable; unavailable form interaction has a localized explanation rather than a false submit action.

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements

- **FR-001 — Locale entry and routes**: The site MUST default `/` to `/ar/`, support English at `/en/`, preserve deliberate locale choice, and offer direct-load/refresh-safe Home, Contact and privacy routes. Arabic MUST use `lang="ar" dir="rtl"`; English MUST use `lang="en" dir="ltr"`. **Acceptance**: Entry, refresh and document language/direction checks pass for both locales without geographic redirection. (P0-F001; constitution III.)
- **FR-002 — Shared navigation**: Header/footer MUST use one six-page route map with localized labels, language switcher and contact/quotation path: locale home, `/about`, `/services`, `/projects`, `/why-ken` and `/contact` under both `/ar/` and `/en/`. Privacy is a utility link outside the six primary items. Deferred destinations MUST show explicit preparation states in preview, not broken links. **Acceptance**: Every displayed navigation/CTA destination resolves correctly in its locale; switch preserves paired page and validated non-personal quotation context. (P0-F001/P0-F006; §4.)
- **FR-003 — Complete bilingual states**: All Phase 1 visible copy, forms, feedback, empty/preparation states, image alternatives and metadata MUST have reviewed Arabic/English variants. **Acceptance**: Missing keys, invalid route pairings, content IDs or media references fail validation; runtime absence shows a localized fallback without raw keys. (P0-F001; III.)
- **FR-004 — Mobile navigation**: Navigation MUST support touch, pointer, keyboard, announced expanded state, Escape and focus restoration; modal navigation, if used, MUST contain focus while open. **Acceptance**: Manual keyboard/screen-reader tests pass in both directions with no hidden focus or unreachable link. (P0-F001/P0-F002; IV.)
- **FR-005 — Initial homepage**: Home MUST expose a legible hero/title, prominent quotation CTA, six service-category entry choices, a differentiator area using approved copy only, honest project-preview state and closing contact CTA. **Acceptance**: Content and CTAs remain readable before media loads; unapproved sections use localized preparation states and no assertions of projects, credentials, coverage or statistics. (P0-F002; I.)
- **FR-006 — Design foundation**: The foundation MUST document provisional dark-navy, brushed-gold and warm-neutral tokens, Arabic/Latin typography, spacing, responsive media and reusable header/footer/hero/button/card/form/section patterns; any modal pattern MUST carry accessibility rules. **Acceptance**: Phase 1 pages consistently use the documented patterns and show default, focus, disabled, pending, success and error treatments where relevant; final colors/fonts/assets remain pending visual sign-off. (P0-F002; IV.)
- **FR-007 — Responsive and resilient display**: All Phase 1 routes MUST preserve reading order, mixed-direction phone/numeric legibility, CTA access and layout under required widths, reduced motion and media/enhancement failures. **Acceptance**: QC-002's matrix passes in both locales with reserved image space, meaningful fallbacks and no motion-dependent content. (P0-F001/P0-F002.)
- **FR-008 — Account-free inquiry modes**: Contact MUST offer general and quotation modes without accounts. **Acceptance**: Both modes can be opened directly and submitted in each locale; quotation shows optional property type, floors and timeline, and never returns an instant price. (P0-F005.)
- **FR-009 — Field contract**: Required fields MUST be name 2–100 characters, valid E.164-normalized phone, message 10–2,000 characters, inquiry type (`general` or `quotation`) and explicit privacy acknowledgement; record supported locale (`ar` or `en`) and current notice version. Email, city and service are optional; property type/floors/timeline are optional quotation details. City MUST NOT become required merely because the villa example includes it. **Acceptance**: Required/optional labels are clear, empty optional values pass, populated email/phone and all configured types/enums/limits are validated; exact boundary tests pass in both modes/locales. (P0-F005; §§11.1/11.3; VII.)
- **FR-010 — Service context**: The only service identities MUST be `villa`, `school`, `administrative`, `restaurant`, `installation`, `maintenance`. Valid quotation context MUST preselect an editable choice; unsafe URL context MUST be ignored. **Acceptance**: Each identity is tested in both locales, including changed/cleared selection and malformed query values; no user-entered personal data is put into URLs. (P0-F003/P0-F006; §11.4.)
- **FR-011 — Accessible form lifecycle**: Forms MUST provide editing/validation, submitting, durable success and recoverable failure states with localized, announced feedback. **Acceptance**: First invalid field and successful confirmation receive focus; concurrent submit is restrained; network, token, rate, conflict and storage failures preserve the draft and provide usable retry. (P0-F005; §11.6; IV/VII.)
- **FR-012 — Independent secure validation**: The submission boundary MUST independently enforce allowlisted fields, types, lengths/enums, total size, human verification and configurable rate limits before accepting a lead. **Acceptance**: Bypassed client validation, missing/expired/invalid verification, provider outage, excessive requests and payload boundary violations insert zero leads; injection-like content cannot execute or expose data. Concrete optional-field bounds, request-size cap and rate/cooldown values MUST be documented during planning and tested below/at/above their boundaries. (P0-F005/P0-F007; VI.)
- **FR-013 — Durable acceptance**: A new accepted submission MUST have a UUID reference and durable creation timestamp before visitor success, independent of email delivery. **Acceptance**: Confirmation reference matches a D1 inquiry retrievable after a fresh operational session; unavailable storage gives recoverable failure rather than acceptance. (P0-F005; §§9/11.4; VII.)
- **FR-014 — Idempotent retry**: Identical concurrent/repeated submissions with the same submission identity MUST return the original reference without duplicate leads; reuse with materially different lead content MUST conflict. The inquiry MUST retain a unique idempotency-key hash and payload hash. **Acceptance**: Lost-response retry, parallel retry and changed-payload scenarios have respectively one, one and no additional records; refreshed verification tokens alone do not constitute changed lead content or a new identity, and retries still satisfy verification/rate controls. Planning MUST define normalized payload comparison and identity lifetime within approved data-retention constraints. (P0-F005; §§9/11.4; VI/VII.)
- **FR-015 — Safe submission responses**: The existing PRD JSON submission contract MUST be preserved, with safe localized feedback and correlation references. **Acceptance**: New acceptance returns 201 with `referenceId` and `status="received"`; identical replay returns 200 with the original reference. Invalid request, identity conflict, rate limit and storage failure use the PRD statuses recorded under inherited constraints; oversized/spam rejection never appears as success or exposes internal errors. Responses MUST NOT echo personal fields/messages or disclose notification-provider internals. (P0-F005/P0-F007; §11.3.)
- **FR-016 — Recorded notifications**: Notification MUST follow persistence and each accepted lead MUST have a linked notification status/attempt record. **Acceptance**: Success and forced failure are traceable to the stored inquiry; delivery failure does not remove the record or change visitor success. Reconciliation finds every persisted inquiry even if interrupted before its first delivery attempt/status update, treating it as pending/unattempted until attempted or explicitly escalated. Phase 1 provider simulation is labeled; provider acceptance is not claimed as proof of inbox receipt. (P0-F005; §10 Phase 1; VII.)
- **FR-017 — Recovery and access**: An authorized retry or documented operational replay MUST recover failed/pending delivery without creating another inquiry. **Acceptance**: Staging replay is demonstrated, daily reconciliation makes failures visible within one business day, and restricted retrieval works for a designated primary and backup operator using least privilege/MFA; unauthorized retrieval is denied. (P0-F005/P0-F007; VI–VIII.)
- **FR-018 — Privacy acknowledgement**: Each mode MUST link a matching-locale notice, require an unchecked-by-default acknowledgement, and record its version/timestamp with acceptance. **Acceptance**: Missing/false acknowledgement or invalid notice version cannot store a lead; only necessary contact/building fields are requested; unapproved notice/data processing permits synthetic testing only. (P0-F005; VI.)
- **FR-019 — Data isolation and confidentiality**: Personal fields, messages, secrets and verification tokens MUST NOT appear in URLs, public caches/configuration, analytics or application logs; public inquiry list/read/update operations MUST NOT exist. **Acceptance**: Negative access tests and inspection of responses, logs, bundles and requests reveal no unauthorized sensitive disclosure; personal fields travel only through the protected submission and approved restricted lead-response/retrieval channels. Operational access uses provider roles, least privilege and MFA. (P0-F005/P0-F007; VI.)
- **FR-020 — Honest direct contact**: Phone/WhatsApp actions MUST use verified company details and explicit user action; when unavailable, the working form MUST remain findable. **Acceptance**: Missing/unverified numbers generate no actionable link; any configured verified action has a localized accessible name, correctly encoded destination and mobile/desktop evidence. Final live-channel rollout remains Phase 2. (P0-F006; I/IV.)
- **FR-021 — Preview deployment**: Phase 1 MUST have a traceable HTTPS preview, documented environment separation, staging D1 storage and server-verified Turnstile. Notification simulation MUST be controlled and labeled; enabled live delivery requires an approved test destination. **Acceptance**: Direct route smoke tests and a stored synthetic lead run on the deployed preview; environment evidence shows separate preview/production storage, verification configuration and, where configured, recipients; missing D1/Turnstile bindings block readiness. A local-only simulation cannot replace the hosted persistence/recovery journey. (P0-F007; §10 Phase 1.)
- **FR-022 — Initial content and discovery foundation**: Implemented Home, Contact and privacy routes MUST serve meaningful localized initial HTML without an extra public-content fetch, distinct title/description, matching canonical and paired language metadata. **Acceptance**: Initial-response inspection and refresh tests pass; unknown URLs return localized HTTP 404s; preview robots directives prevent indexing of draft/test content. Complete production SEO remains a later gate. (P0-F001/P0-F007; II/V.)
- **FR-023 — Quality checks and evidence**: Preview readiness MUST depend on recorded critical automated and manual checks, reproducible fixtures, performance budgets and a reviewed CI baseline. **Acceptance**: QC-003/QC-006 evidence links to the candidate revision; a failing mandatory check prevents ready status; external-provider simulations are clearly separated from live staging results. (P0-F007; VIII.)
- **FR-024 — Operational readiness foundation**: Phase 1 MUST document lead retrieval/replay, escalation, test-data cleanup, safe monitoring, deployment rollback and outstanding release procedures/owners. **Acceptance**: QC-007's alert/recovery demonstration and preview rollback exercise pass; backup/export/restore feasibility is recorded without claiming approval of proposed retention periods. (P0-F007; VI–VIII.)
- **FR-025 — Dependency and approval register**: Missing assets, credentials, contacts, assignments, privacy clearance and production prerequisites MUST have owner roles and gates. **Acceptance**: Every dependency below has a status, consequence and deadline/gate; Phase 1 readiness never implies PRD approval, real-data authorization or launch authorization. (PRD §§4, 14–16; I/VIII.)

### Quality and Compliance Requirements *(mandatory)*

- **QC-001 — Localization (III)**: Review 100% of Phase 1 strings, alt text and metadata in Arabic and English. Acceptance includes language/direction attributes, page-preserving switch, logical layouts, directional icons, breadcrumb order where present, keyboard focus order and legible Latin phone/numeric content. A native-speaking reviewer for each locale records approval or the review gate remains pending.
- **QC-002 — Premium UX/accessibility (IV)**: Target WCAG 2.1 AA on Home, Contact, privacy and shell states. Evidence MUST include semantic landmarks/headings, associated labels/errors, screen-reader announcements, visible focus and keyboard access. Text contrast MUST reach 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text; applicable controls/focus indicators meet AA non-text contrast. Required widths are 360, 390, 768, 1024 and 1440 CSS pixels in both locales, with no horizontal overflow/clipped RTL text. Targets are at least 44 × 44 CSS pixels where practicable; any exception is documented. Reduced motion suppresses parallax, lengthy/autoplay-style movement; no scroll hijacking/hover-only actions. Lighthouse accessibility MUST be ≥95 per implemented representative template/locale, with zero critical manual keyboard or screen-reader failures; scores alone do not establish compliance.
- **QC-003 — Discovery/performance (II/V)**: Phase 1 MUST define and enforce compressed per-route asset budgets during planning. Home/Contact MUST achieve Lighthouse mobile performance ≥90 using an agreed repeatable throttled profile, with critical homepage content usable within two seconds where practicable and approved exceptions recorded. Record LCP ≤2.5 seconds, INP ≤200 ms and CLS ≤0.10 as release baselines; prelaunch synthetic evidence MUST name device/network/cache/tool settings and distinguish lab measurements from field p75. Insufficient field data MUST be reported as such. Media MUST reserve dimensions, use responsive modern derivatives with JPEG fallback, load critical hero promptly and lazy-load below-fold assets; local licensed fonts and lightweight posters take precedence over blocking video/scripts. All 12 primary URLs, legal content, sitemap, robots, canonical/hreflang, social metadata and verified structured data remain mandatory release checks as later routes/content arrive.
- **QC-004 — Security/privacy (VI)**: Test independent validation, human-verification fail-closed behavior, size/rate controls, parameterized storage handling, secure transport, secret isolation and unauthorized access rejection. Both locale forms MUST expose notice/acknowledgement behavior; no raw personal data, message text or tokens in logs/analytics/caches. Legal review of lawful basis, Saudi PDPL, processing/subprocessor locations, transfers and retention MUST begin in Phase 1; this spec makes no legal-compliance determination. No real customer data is processed in unapproved infrastructure.
- **QC-005 — Lead reliability (VII)**: Every accepted synthetic lead MUST remain retrievable after notification failure and have reference, creation time, locale, notice acknowledgement and delivery status. Duplicate/conflict, storage-outage, notification-outage and interrupted-delivery tests MUST prove no silent lead loss or duplicate inquiry. Daily reconciliation and one-business-day visibility MUST be demonstrated through authorized retrieval/replay. Production baseline is ≥99.5% durable acceptance for valid non-spam attempts over a 30-day window, not a claim derived from short preview tests.
- **QC-006 — Verification (VIII)**: Mandatory checks include formatting, lint, type checks, meaningful unit/integration tests, route/content validation, accessibility smoke checks and production build. Automated evidence MUST cover both modes/locales, exact required-field boundaries, malformed/oversized/spam/rate rejection, concurrent duplicates/conflicts, storage failure and notification recovery. Manual preview review MUST cover keyboard/screen reader, reduced motion, responsive layouts and editorial correctness in both locales; browser matrix is latest two major Chrome, Firefox, Safari and Edge plus current iOS Safari and Android Chrome, recording actual versions at execution. Missing device/required hosted-service access is reported as a blocked check. Labeled notification-provider simulation is permitted for Phase 1 recovery; live delivery remains a later recorded gate. Full production smoke, backup/restore and release acceptance are deferred obligations, never treated as passed by the preview.
- **QC-007 — Operations (VI–VIII)**: Safe logs MUST expose correlation references, validation category and notification status without personal fields. Demonstrate alert behavior for >1% server errors in 15 minutes and sustained notification failure; planning MUST define the sustained-failure trigger and escalation recipients. Runbook MUST name primary/backup authorized operators, daily reconciliation and retry/escalation steps; demonstrate preview rollback to a known revision without discarding stored leads. Record feasibility/owners for approved restricted encrypted backups, deletion/export and restore drills before launch. Carry monthly availability ≥99.9% and unexpected backend errors <0.1% as future operational baselines, excluding deliberate failure tests from normal error metrics.

#### Inherited Technology and Contract Constraints

This subsection preserves existing PRD/constitution constraints at the user's explicit request; it introduces no implementation design or vendor selection. Planning MUST retain React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, React Router or a compatible equivalent, shared typed bilingual source content, and build-time prerendering/SSG. Hosting/runtime remain Cloudflare Pages and minimal Pages Functions under `/api/v1/`; migration-managed Cloudflare D1 stores inquiries/notification attempts subject to privacy clearance; Turnstile is verified server-side. No standalone Express server, visitor JWT or user table is required. Environments are local, preview/staging and production with isolated D1, Turnstile and recipients. GitHub Actions is proposed, and the transactional email provider remains TBD. Unapproved regions/providers cannot be silently substituted or treated as approved.

Both form modes use the existing `POST /api/v1/inquiries` contract: 201 for new durable acceptance, 200 for identical replay with the original reference, 400 for invalid input, 409 for conflicting identity/payload, 429 for rate limit, and 503 for storage failure. Public GET resources expose approved content/configuration only and are not needed for initial content rendering; public inquiry reads/updates are prohibited. Planning decides implementation paths, optional-field bounds, payload/rate budgets and recovery tooling within these constraints.

Phase 1 completion requires passing applicable hosted preview evidence, Phase 1 wireframe/route/content-model/provisional-token approval, reviewed visible bilingual copy and authorized recovery access, while recording outstanding later gates. Pending Phase 1 approvals allow provisional review but MUST NOT be reported as completed acceptance. Labeled provider simulation can satisfy the Phase 1 failure/recovery demonstration, not live email delivery. Public launch still requires all PRD P0 acceptance, complete bilingual pages/services/genuine gallery, approved brand/content/contacts, legal and data-location clearance, production lead/notification smoke tests, SEO/accessibility/performance acceptance, backup/restore and rollback drills, and KEN Management authorization after Product Owner/QA acceptance. None is waived by this specification.

### Key Entities *(include if feature involves data)*

- **Localized Page / Route Pair**: Logical page identity, Arabic/English destinations, approved or preparation-state content, localized titles/descriptions and publication state; supports page-preserving switching.
- **Design System**: Provisional visual tokens, typography licenses, reusable interaction patterns and approval status; governs consistent Phase 1 presentation.
- **Public Site Configuration / Media Asset**: Approved non-sensitive company details and contact channels; image dimensions, bilingual alternatives, rights and editorial-versus-real-project classification. Unverified facts are omitted.
- **Service Context**: One of six stable service identities, localized label and editable quotation preselection; detailed service content is deferred.
- **Inquiry**: UUID reference, created time, locale, general/quotation type, name, normalized phone, message, optional contact/building context, privacy version/acknowledgement time, unique idempotency-key hash, payload hash and processing state; authoritative accepted lead record in migration-managed D1.
- **Notification Attempt**: Belongs to one inquiry; provider, pending/successful/failed delivery state, attempt count/time, next retry and safe error category/escalation information; multiple attempts may belong to one inquiry. Simulation versus live delivery is identified in evidence; recovery includes inquiries awaiting their first attempt without copying personal data into logs.
- **Privacy Notice / Approval Dependency**: Versioned bilingual notice and acknowledgement rules; owner, status, required gate and approval evidence for privacy, content, vendor and release dependencies.
- **Verification Evidence**: Candidate revision/preview address, locale, browser/device/profile, synthetic-versus-live test designation, outcome and unresolved dependency; contains no raw lead personal data.

## Success Criteria *(mandatory)*

### Measurable Outcomes

- **SC-001 — Reach and language parity**: 100% of Phase 1 paired routes load directly and survive refresh; 100% of language-switch cases preserve the corresponding page and safe quotation context; `/` always opens Arabic and explicit English links stay English.
- **SC-002 — Complete readable foundation**: 100% of Phase 1 public strings, alternative text and metadata have reviewed variants; all five required viewport widths pass in both locales with zero overflow/clipped Arabic text and every visible CTA reachable by keyboard/touch without motion.
- **SC-003 — Inquiry task completion**: In at least ten representative residential/commercial synthetic quotation tests spanning both languages, ≥85% complete without assistance; a prepared villa-buyer scenario completes in under three minutes. QA/Product Owner record sample split, attempts and timing; fixture tasks do not imply full service-page completion.
- **SC-004 — Durable acceptance**: 100% of valid general/quotation scenarios in both locales receive a reference matching exactly one retrievable stored record. All identical retry/concurrency cases reuse the original reference; all conflicting-key cases create zero additional leads.
- **SC-005 — Safe rejection**: 100% of invalid-input, oversized, failed-verification and rate-limited test cases create zero leads and return safe localized feedback; every recoverable form-failure case retains entered data and never presents false stored success.
- **SC-006 — Notification recovery**: 100% of forced notification-failure/interruption cases preserve visitor acceptance and stored inquiry; every affected lead is visible to the designated operator within one business day, and recovery/replay creates zero duplicate inquiries.
- **SC-007 — Accessibility and speed**: Every implemented representative Home/Contact/privacy template in each locale achieves accessibility audit ≥95 with zero critical manual keyboard/screen-reader failures. Home/Contact achieve mobile performance audit ≥90 and homepage critical content is usable within two seconds on the agreed synthetic profile, unless an approved practicability exception is recorded. Measured prelaunch loading/stability/interaction results are assessed against 2.5 seconds/0.10/200 ms baselines, clearly labeled as lab evidence rather than field percentiles.
- **SC-008 — Perceived trust/contact clarity**: At least ten moderated testers spanning both locales rate the preview's trust and ease of finding contact at mean ≥4.5/5. Unapproved company claims, project evidence and actionable placeholder contact numbers total zero.
- **SC-009 — Preview proof**: One traceable hosted preview demonstrates the mobile Arabic entry → English switch → valid stored test inquiry → authorized retrieval journey, plus invalid rejection and failed-notification recovery. All mandatory candidate checks and Phase 1 approval gates pass and every outstanding later dependency has an owner role, status and gate. Notification-provider simulation is labeled and permitted for recovery proof; hosted D1/Turnstile and authorized retrieval require actual integration evidence, and live email remains a later gate.
- **SC-010 — Confidentiality and isolation**: All negative public-access tests disclose zero inquiry records; all reviewed bundles/logs/URLs/cache/analytics outputs disclose zero secrets or raw personal fields/messages/tokens; staging demonstrations create zero production records or unintended production notifications.

These are feature acceptance targets, not completed test results. PRD §12 thresholds remain subject to stakeholder sign-off. Thirty-day reliability/availability and day-90 business metrics require later operation; they are not Phase 1 pass claims. Proposed goals of ≥20 qualified inquiries/month, ≥3% visitor-to-lead conversion and ≥5% direct-contact intent remain later measurement goals, excluding bots/test events. Missing session data is reported as insufficient data, never an invented conversion rate.
